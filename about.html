About Pradeep Fashion

Celebrating Indian ethnic wear with authenticity, elegance, and care.

Pradeep Fashion Bangalore is a trusted wholesale destination for premium Indian ethnic wear. We specialize in exclusive collections of fancy kurtis and ready-made churidars, designed to meet the evolving demands of modern retailers.

Located in Bangalore, we bring together tradition and contemporary fashion by offering thoughtfully curated designs that suit festive occasions, daily wear, and special celebrations.

Our collections are selected with a strong focus on superior fabric quality, fine detailing, comfortable fits, and elegant finishing — ensuring every piece reflects reliability and refined craftsmanship.

By partnering with trusted manufacturers and suppliers, we maintain consistency in design, quality, and delivery, making us a dependable choice for retailers seeking premium ethnic fashion.

At Pradeep Fashion Bangalore, our mission is to provide stylish, high-quality ethnic wear that helps businesses grow while celebrating India’s rich cultural heritage.
